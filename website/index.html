About us

Founded to make complicated relationships easier to understand.

KinForge was created after studying genealogy apps, family-tree websites, creator tools, reviews, accessibility gaps and confusing user experiences. The goal is to keep the depth people need, but make the interface calmer, clearer and easier to use.

Founding Story

KinForge exists because many existing tools felt too narrow, too expensive, too crowded, too slow, or not inclusive enough for users with complex relationship maps.

Read the founding story →

Mission

Help people understand families, cases, characters, histories, communities and fictional worlds through clear relationship mapping and readable reports.

Vision

Become the relationship studio people open when a simple list is not enough and the story, case, research or world needs real structure.

Values

Clarity, accessibility, privacy, inclusion, careful evidence, creator freedom, and respect for sensitive real-life information.

Accessibility

Plain-language explanations, readable layouts, disability-aware records, low-vision and dyslexia-friendly thinking, and calmer navigation.

Who it serves

Social workers, writers, authors, genealogists, historians, roleplayers, RPG players, educators, researchers, illustrators and worldbuilders.

Built for more than family trees

KinForge is for social workers mapping care context, writers building casts and series continuity, genealogists researching family lines, historians tracking people and evidence, and roleplayers or RPG players managing worlds and campaigns.

Relationship clarity

Follow parents, partners, siblings, guardians, descendants and other important links in a connected chart.

Project structure

Keep books, cases, collections, subcollections, campaigns and trees together in your library.

Readable outputs

Use reports, timelines, tutorials and support resources to understand complicated relationships.
